      Project 05 · Major projectMedical AI · Research

      In progressNov 2026 – May 2027 · team of two
      Ongoing · no results yet

      Medical Cross-Attention Transformer using Agentic AI

      A research prototype for skin-lesion classification that fuses dermoscopic images with patient metadata through bidirectional cross-attention in a ViT/Swin backbone. It is designed to keep working when metadata is missing, to report calibrated uncertainty with MC dropout and temperature scaling, and to refer low-confidence cases instead of guessing. A LangGraph agent running a local LLM through Ollama asks for the missing information that would help most.

      Status: design and data preparation. Baselines on PAD-UFES-20 and HAM10000 are planned next, with patient-level 5-fold cross-validation. I will publish numbers only once they exist. Research prototype only, not a diagnostic tool and not for clinical use.
